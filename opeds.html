Op-eds

I write a regular column in Morgenbladet and contribute op-eds to other Norwegian and international outlets, mostly on democracy and autocracy, war, the politics of science and higher education, and artificial intelligence. Titles are given in the original language; the short summaries are in English. Most pieces are behind the publishers’ paywalls.
